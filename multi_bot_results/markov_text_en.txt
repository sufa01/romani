A Psalm of the Sons of Korah. Lord, you were favorable to your land; you restored the fortunes of Jacob. You forgave the iniquity of your people; you covered all their sin. Selah You withdrew all your wrath; you turned from your hot anger. Restore us again, O God of our salvation, and put away your indignation toward us! Will you be angry forever? Will your jealousy burn like fire? Pour out your indignation upon them, and let your burning anger overtake them. May their camp be a desolation; let no one dwell in their tents. For they persecute him whom you have made summer and winter. Remember this, O Lord, how the enemy scoffs, and a foolish people reviles your name. Do not deliver the soul of your servant, for to you, O Lord, do I lift up my hands. My soul will be satisfied as with fat and rich food, and my mouth will praise you yet more and more. My mouth will tell of your righteous help all the day long I have been as a portent to many, but you are my rock and my salvation, my fortress; I shall not be afraid. What can man do to me? I must perform my vows day after day. 19.62. Chapter 62 (My Soul Waits for God Alone)To the choirmaster: according to Do Not Destroy. A Psalm of David. A Song. Praise is due to you, O Lord, laugh at them; you hold all the nations in derision. O my Strength, I will watch for you, for you, O God, have tested us; you have tried us as silver is tried. You brought us into the net; you laid a crushing burden on our backs; you let men ride over our heads; we went through fire and through water; yet you have brought us out to a place of springs; the early rain also covers it with pools. They go from strength to strength; each one appears before God in the land. We do not see our signs; there is no foothold; I have come into your inheritance; they have defiled your holy temple; they have laid Jerusalem in ruins. They have given the bodies of your servants to the birds of the heavens for food, the flesh of your faithful to the beasts of the earth. You divided the sea by your might; you broke the heads of Leviathan; you gave him as food for the creatures of the wilderness. You split open springs and brooks; you dried up ever-flowing streams. Yours is the day, yours also the night; you have established the heavenly lights and the sun. You have fixed all the boundaries of the earth; you have made strong for yourself! Then we shall not turn back to them, and find no fault in them. And they say, "How can God know? Is there knowledge in the Most High?" Behold, these are the wicked; always at ease, they increase in riches. All in vain have I kept my heart clean and washed my hands in innocence. For all the day long, for they have been put to shame and dismayed forever; let them perish in disgrace, that they may know that you alone, whose name is the Lord, are the Most High in the desert. They tested God again and again and provoked the Holy One of Israel. My lips will shout for joy, when I sing praises to you, for you, O God, when the waters saw you, they were afraid; indeed, the deep trembled. The clouds poured out water; the skies gave forth thunder; your arrows flashed on every side. The crash of your thunder was in the wilderness and gave them drink abundantly as from the deep. He made streams come out of the land are full of the habitations of violence. Let not the downtrodden turn back in shame; let the poor and him who has no helper. He has pity on the weak and the needy, and crush the oppressor! May they fear you while the sun endures, and as long as the sun! May people be blessed in him, all nations serve him! For he delivers the needy when he calls, the poor and needy praise your name. Arise, O God, judge the earth; for you shall inherit all the nations! 19.83.
